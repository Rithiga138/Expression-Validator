Expression Notations

The same calculation can be written three ways. The position of the operator is what differs.

Infix

A + B

Operator appears between operands.

Example: (25 + 15) * 2

Prefix

+ A B

Operator appears before operands.

Example: * + 25 15 2

Postfix

A B +

Operator appears after operands.

Example: 25 15 + 2 *
